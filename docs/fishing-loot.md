# Fishing loot rules

Use **/cwp admin → Build → Loot pools** to create a pool and set its item or currency rewards, amounts, chances, and nested pools. Then open **Build → Fishing** and add a biome or pocket. Each biome or pocket appears once in the Fishing list. Open it to add, edit, or remove any number of rulesets. Removing the last ruleset leaves the biome or pocket available for another ruleset; deleting the biome or pocket removes it and all of its rulesets. Existing saved rules are grouped under their biome or pocket automatically.

Each ruleset inherits its biome or pocket from the parent entry and can constrain a catch by liquid, weather, and time. All conditions must match. Leave liquid blank for any liquid. Type a plain name such as `river`, `water`, or `deep_cave` and choose an autocomplete result. A pocket name matches catches inside that pocket's workshop and running instances, regardless of the biome placed there. External mod IDs remain explicit, for example `othermod:glowing_river`. New `#` tag rules are no longer accepted. The editor suggests registered biome and fluid IDs, pocket names, and loot pools while typing. Flowing vanilla water and lava match their source IDs. Weather is `any`, `clear`, `rain`, or `thunder` at the bobber. Time is `any`, `day`, or `night`; night is the bobber dimension's default world clock ticks 13000–22999.

When several rulesets match, one is selected according to its weight. Inside a pocket, matching pocket rulesets take precedence over biome rulesets. For example, matching weights 90 and 10 select those rulesets 90% and 10% of the time. Only the selected ruleset's pool rolls; entries inside that pool retain their independent chances, so one catch can still produce several items. The rewards replace the vanilla fishing catch. Item rewards launch from the bobber as ordinary caught items; player currency rewards are credited directly. If no ruleset matches, vanilla fishing loot remains. A pool that is in use by a fishing ruleset cannot be deleted until the ruleset is removed or changed.

For example, a general `dark_forest` ruleset with weight 90 and a night-only `dark_forest` ruleset with weight 10 both match at night. The night pool is selected for 10% of those catches. During the day, the general pool is the only match and is always selected.

Item entries also have optional **Fishing XP per item** and **Level** metadata. A fish configured with XP value 5 would be worth 5 fishing XP per item when the future stat system is connected. Level will require the player's fishing stat to meet or exceed that value. Both values are saved now; no XP or level restriction is applied yet.

The current vanilla fishing rod still follows vanilla water-only bite behavior. A future lava-capable rod or bobber can call `FishingLootService.catchAt(ServerLevel, ServerPlayer, BlockPos, FluidState)` when it resolves a catch. A `null` return means use the rod's default loot; an empty returned list means a matching custom pool produced no item. The rod must supply its own lava bite and bobber behavior.
